## About Me

**EHR Analyst with 5+ years of professional experience**, including two years supporting **MEDITECH Expanse** and **Epic** EHR implementations and application support at Tegria.

I work where clinical workflows meet system configuration — translating how care actually gets delivered into builds that hold up on go-live day.

**Epic implementation — San Bernardino County DPH.** Translated public-health clinic and lab processes (immunization clinics, client intake, mobile outreach) into Epic configuration and build. Mapped current-state workflows into future-state Epic designs, coordinated interfaces across scheduling, lab, and state immunization registries during migration off legacy Athena/CPSI, ran clinical and user acceptance testing, and delivered at-the-elbow go-live support.

**MEDITECH Expanse (MaaS) implementation — Huggins Hospital.** Workflow validation, issue tracking, and go-live readiness across clinical and patient-access workflows. Supported migration of scheduling and patient data from legacy Allscripts and CPSI, consolidation of the MyHealth patient portal, and clean-claim workflow reviews contributing to a **90% clean-claims rate**.

Currently pursuing an **M.S. in Health Informatics** at Benedictine University.

**Open to:** EHR Analyst · Clinical Informatics Analyst · Application Analyst · Healthcare Data Analyst · Revenue Cycle Analyst — onsite, hybrid, or remote, with relocation anywhere in the United States.
